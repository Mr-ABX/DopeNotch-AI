How to Open DopeNotch AI (First Launch)

DopeNotch runs 100% locally on your Mac. While our Apple notarization is finishing up, follow these quick steps once to open it.

1

Drag DopeNotch to Applications

Drag DopeNotch.app into your Applications folder, then open it from Applications.

2

Right-Click → Open (or Approve in Privacy & Security)

Fastest way: In Applications, Right-Click DopeNotch → click Open.
On macOS Sequoia: If macOS shows a “Not Opened” popup, do these 4 clicks:

A. Click “Done” on the popup
B. System Settings → Privacy & Security → “Open Anyway”
C. Click “Open Anyway” to confirm
D. Approve with Touch ID or Mac Password
Done! DopeNotch AI will now open normally every time. Official site: dopenotch.xbuilds.online · Help: dopenotch@xbuilds.online
